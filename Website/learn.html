LEARN CHORDS

  • C major

    The C major chord is one of the most fundamental and harmonious chords in music, built from the notes C, E and G. It features the root, major third, and perfect fifth, producing a bright and stable sound. This chord forms the backbone of countless songs across genres

  • D major

    The D major chord is built from the notes D, F# and A. It features the root, major third, and perfect fifth, producing a bright and stable sound. This chord is commonly used in many popular songs.

  • E minor

    The E minor chord is built from the notes E, G and B. It features the root, minor third, and perfect fifth, producing a somber and introspective sound. This chord is commonly used in many popular songs.
